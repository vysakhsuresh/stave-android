# The five we are built to beat

Read off their listings, help centres and reviews on 31 October 2026; **nothing here was seen
by driving the apps**. The full reading, with our answer to each, is section 2 and 3 of
`STAVE_BRIEF.md`. The one-line summary per app:

| App | Their strength | Their weakness we answer |
|---|---|---|
| BandLab | Free 16-track DAW, effects, Splitter, mastering, social | Account, cloud, 15-minute cap, upload for stems, ads and a moving paywall |
| Audio Evolution Mobile | The deepest recorder: automation on everything, USB interfaces | A paid app with paid add-ons and a paid USB driver; no AI; a dated UI |
| FL Studio Mobile | Beats: sequencer, piano roll, instruments; `.flp` to desktop | Paid plus paid sound packs; weak wave editing; nothing AI |
| Moises | Stems, chords, key, BPM, practice | Every separation uploaded; monthly caps across three tiers; edits nothing |
| Lexis Audio Editor | The most-installed plain editor; noise reduction, EQ, tempo/pitch | One track, 16-bit, a spectrogram you cannot touch, MP3 behind the paywall |

The opinion from Squish's competitor doc holds: do not chase parity, chase the thing none of
them can do. For Stave that is: everything on-device, nothing capped, one app across all five
jobs, and the arithmetic executed and checked.
